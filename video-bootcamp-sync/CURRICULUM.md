# AI Video Bootcamp — known curriculum structure

Phase-level structure, sourced from Skool's own public marketing page
(`aivideobootcamp.com/curriculum/`) — legitimately public, not from behind
the Skool login wall. Confirmed 2026-07-02.

Lesson-level detail (titles, count, and order *within* each phase) is
**not published anywhere public** and only exists behind Skool login —
searched for it (search engines, the community's own site, third-party
directories) and it genuinely isn't out there. That part can only come
from `skool_sync.py`/`skool_http.py` once run against a real logged-in
session (see PLAN.md). Treat the phases below as the real `<Module>`
folder names to use in `01-Video/`, replacing the placeholder
`01-Module-Example` from the earlier POC.

| # | Phase | Covers |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Initiate | Navigating the course & community, tool overview |
| 2 | AI Images | Prompting, composition, lighting, editing for professional visuals |
| 3 | AI Videos | Static image → cinematic video, motion & camera control |
| 4 | Sound Effects & Editing | Sound design, voice, music, editing |
| 5 | Character Consistency | Face/outfit/environment locking, story-driven content |
| 6 | AI Video Ads | AI influencers, UGC ads, product campaigns |
| 7 | Social Media AI Content | TikTok/Instagram/YouTube optimization, viral formats |
| 8 | AI Filmmaking | Cinematic storytelling, dialogue, pacing |
| 9 | Cloning & Automation | Face/voice cloning, scaled production systems |

Plus prompt libraries, templates, and weekly tool updates layered across
all phases (per the marketing copy — not phase-specific).

Third-party "free download" / course-reseller sites claiming to list a
full module/lesson breakdown were deliberately not used as a source here
— using pirated listings to reconstruct paywalled content isn't
something this plan relies on, even where such sites are indexed
publicly.
